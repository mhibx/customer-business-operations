
# Campaign Operations

This section documents selected campaign planning and execution work from my previous professional experience.

The work covered sales and promotional campaigns designed to drive transactions, customer engagement, and platform activity.

The available portfolio evidence includes campaign objectives, promotional creatives, execution examples, and documented results.

## Overview

My campaign operations work involved planning, coordinating, executing, and monitoring sales and promotional campaigns.

Key activities included:

- Campaign planning and execution
- Sales and promotional initiatives
- Promotional offer communication
- Customer engagement activities
- Coordination with marketing and operational activities
- Campaign performance monitoring

The original portfolio describes this work as designing, planning, and executing sales and promotional campaigns to drive sales to the platform.
