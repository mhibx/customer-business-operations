
# Customer Engagement

This section documents selected professional work involving customer onboarding, user activation, CRM communication, transaction journey engagement, and purchase lifecycle activities.

The work documented here comes from my previous professional experience and is based on the available portfolio evidence from that period.

## Overview

My customer engagement work focused on guiding users through different stages of their customer journey using structured communication workflows.

The activities covered the journey from user registration and onboarding to first purchase, transaction engagement, and subsequent lifecycle communication.

These workflows used multiple communication channels to deliver relevant messages and encourage users to complete their intended actions.

## Areas of Work

### User Onboarding & Activation

Developed and managed communication workflows for newly registered users.

The objective was to guide users through the initial stages of their customer journey and encourage their first meaningful interaction with the platform.

Key activities included:

- User onboarding
- Activation communication
- First-purchase engagement
- Customer lifecycle communication
- Multi-channel engagement

### Transaction Journey Engagement

Worked on communication workflows for users entering the transaction process.

These workflows supported user engagement and purchase completion through relevant communication and follow-up activities.

Key activities included:

- Transaction journey communication
- Customer follow-up
- Purchase completion support
- Lifecycle automation

### Purchase Journey & Retention

Worked with customer communication across different stages of the purchase journey.

The workflows supported engagement beyond initial registration and purchase, with a focus on customer lifecycle, retention, and conversion.
