# Digital Business Operations

This section documents my experience managing the operational processes of Insider Top Up, a digital gaming top-up business.

My responsibilities covered transaction monitoring, product management, supplier coordination, customer support, reseller operations, and business performance monitoring.

## Overview

Operating a digital product business requires coordination between customers, payment services, internal systems, and product suppliers.

At Insider Top Up, I managed business operations involving the order lifecycle, payment confirmation, supplier fulfillment, transaction monitoring, and issue resolution.

The operational process was supported by website integrations, payment gateway services, supplier APIs, and internal monitoring workflows.

## 1. Order & Transaction Operations

The transaction process involved several connected systems, from customer checkout to supplier fulfillment.

### Transaction Workflow

1. **Product Selection** — Customers select the digital product they want to purchase from the website.
2. **Checkout** — Customers enter the required account information and proceed with checkout.
3. **Payment Processing** — Customers complete payment through the integrated payment gateway.
4. **Payment Confirmation** — The payment gateway sends payment confirmation to the business system.
5. **Supplier Processing** — The system forwards the transaction to the supplier through the Digiflazz API.
6. **Fulfillment** — The supplier processes the digital top-up request.
7. **Transaction Update** — The system receives the supplier response and updates the transaction status.
8. **Customer Notification** — Customers receive transaction status information.

### Operational Focus

- Monitoring incoming orders.
- Tracking payment and transaction statuses.
- Monitoring supplier fulfillment.
- Identifying delayed or failed transactions.
- Supporting customers when transactions encounter problems.

## 3. Issue & Refund Management

Transaction issues can occur at different stages of the order lifecycle, including payment processing and supplier fulfillment.

### Issue Handling Process

1. Identify the affected transaction.
2. Review the transaction status and available records.
3. Check payment confirmation and supplier response.
4. Investigate the cause of the issue.
5. Coordinate with the relevant supplier or service provider when necessary.
6. Communicate the progress to the customer.
7. Follow up until the issue is resolved.

### Common Operational Issues

- Payment confirmation delays.
- Pending supplier transactions.
- Failed digital top-up fulfillment.
- Incorrect or incomplete transaction information.
- Customer complaints regarding transaction status.
- Refund requests related to unsuccessful transactions.

### Refund Handling

Refund requests require transaction verification and coordination with the relevant payment service.

The refund process includes reviewing transaction details, confirming the issue, coordinating the necessary action, and updating the customer regarding the resolution.
