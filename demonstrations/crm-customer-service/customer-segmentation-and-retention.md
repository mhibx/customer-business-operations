# Customer Segmentation & Retention

## Overview

Transaction and engagement information can be used to understand customers beyond individual transactions.

The following segments are used in this demonstration.

---

## 01 — New Customer

A customer who has completed their first transaction.

### CRM Focus

- Smooth onboarding
- Clear product information
- Fast issue resolution

---

## 02 — Repeat Customer

A customer who purchases periodically.

### CRM Focus

- Consistent service
- Transaction convenience
- Customer retention

---

## 03 — High-Frequency Customer

A customer with a high transaction frequency.

### CRM Focus

- Personalized communication
- Faster support
- Retention

---

## 04 — Inactive Customer

A customer who was previously active but has not transacted for a defined period.

### CRM Focus

- Re-engagement
- Relevant promotion
- Understanding potential reasons for inactivity

---

# Customer Retention

Retention should not be viewed only as a promotional problem.

In a digital top-up context, repeat purchase can also be influenced by:

- Transaction speed
- Product availability
- Price competitiveness
- Reliability
- Customer support
- Issue resolution
- Ease of transaction

## Complaint → Retention Insight

A recurring complaint can be treated as a customer-experience signal.

```text
Individual Complaint
        ↓
Recurring Issue
        ↓
Root Cause
        ↓
Process Improvement
```

For example, repeated payment problems may require investigation into:

- Payment gateway issues
- Processing delays
- Customer misunderstanding
- Transaction-status synchronization
- Internal processes

The objective is to improve the underlying experience rather than only close individual tickets.
