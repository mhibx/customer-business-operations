# Recurring Issue Analysis & Customer Service Metrics

> **Important:** All numbers in this document are simulated portfolio data.

## 01 — Simulated Issue Dataset

| Issue | Tickets | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Payment Pending | 35 | 35% |
| Top-Up Delayed | 28 | 28% |
| Wrong User ID | 17 | 17% |
| Product Inquiry | 12 | 12% |
| Other | 8 | 8% |

### Finding

In this simulated dataset, Payment Pending is the most frequent issue category.

### Investigation Areas

Rather than resolving each ticket independently, possible causes can be investigated:

- Payment gateway issue
- Processing delay
- Customer misunderstanding
- Transaction status synchronization
- Internal process

The result can then inform customer communication or internal process improvement.

# 02 — Customer Service Metrics

## First Response Time

How long a customer waits before receiving the first response.

## Resolution Time

How long it takes to resolve the customer's issue.

## Resolution Rate

The percentage of tickets successfully resolved.

## Escalation Rate

The percentage of cases requiring assistance from another team or function.

## Customer Satisfaction

A measure of the customer's experience after receiving support.

# 04 — From Metrics to Action

Metrics should support decisions rather than exist only as reporting numbers.

```text
Metric / Ticket Data
        ↓
Identify Pattern
        ↓
Investigate Cause
        ↓
Define Improvement
        ↓
Measure Again
```

This creates a feedback loop between Customer Service, CRM, and Operations.
