# Customer Journey

## Overview

This document describes the customer journey used in the CRM and Customer Service demonstration.

```text
Customer Discovery
        ↓
Product / Price Inquiry
        ↓
Order & Payment
        ↓
Transaction Processing
        ↓
Issue Resolution
        ↓
Follow-up
        ↓
Repeat Purchase
```

## 01 — Customer Discovery

The customer first discovers the product or service.

The customer-service focus is to provide clear information and help the customer understand the available product.

## 02 — Product / Price Inquiry

The customer may ask about:

- Product availability
- Product type
- Price
- Transaction process
- Other purchase-related information

The objective is to reduce uncertainty before the customer places an order.

## 03 — Order & Payment

The customer submits an order and completes payment.

Relevant information may include:

- Transaction ID
- Product
- Amount
- Payment status
- Customer identifier

## 04 — Transaction Processing

The order is processed and the digital product is delivered.

If the customer reports a problem, the first step is to establish the actual transaction state rather than immediately assuming that the transaction failed.

## 05 — Issue Resolution

Potential issues include:

- Payment pending
- Top-up delayed
- Wrong player ID
- Product inquiry
- Delivery problem

The support process should determine where the problem occurred before selecting the resolution.

## 06 — Follow-up

After an investigation or escalation, the customer should receive an appropriate update.

Follow-up is particularly important when the issue cannot be resolved immediately.

## 07 — Repeat Purchase

A positive and reliable transaction experience can support future purchases.

Customer retention is therefore connected not only to promotional activity, but also to transaction reliability, ease of use, and issue resolution.

## Customer Service Principle

> Do not make the customer repeat the same information multiple times.

Information already provided by the customer should be used to make the investigation more efficient.
