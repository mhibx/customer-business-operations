# Knowledge Base & Process Improvement

## 01 — Knowledge Base Examples

### Q: What should be done if payment succeeded but the top-up has not arrived?

**A:** Check the transaction number, payment status, product, and customer/game ID. After the transaction status is established, provide the customer with the appropriate next step.

---

### Q: What if the customer entered the wrong game ID?

**A:** Check whether the transaction has already been processed. If it has not, follow the applicable procedure for changing the information. If it has already been completed, explain the transaction status based on the available information and applicable service policy.

---

### Q: How should an angry customer be handled?

**A:** Do not respond emotionally. Acknowledge the issue, collect the required information, investigate the transaction, and provide a clear solution or update.

---

# 02 — Complaint Handling

A structured response can follow:

```text
Acknowledge
    ↓
Clarify
    ↓
Investigate
    ↓
Update
    ↓
Resolve / Escalate
    ↓
Follow-up
```

### Poor Response

> "Mohon tunggu ya Kak."

The response does not explain what is being investigated or what the customer should expect next.

### Better Approach

> "Mohon maaf atas kendalanya, Kak. Saya bantu cek transaksinya terlebih dahulu. Mohon kirimkan nomor transaksi atau bukti pembayaran agar saya bisa memastikan statusnya. Setelah saya cek, saya informasikan hasilnya dan langkah berikutnya."

# 03 — CRM Improvement Example

## Problem

Customers frequently ask about transaction status.

## Observation

Customers may not know whether a transaction is:

- Processing
- Successful
- Failed
- Experiencing another issue

## Improvement

Provide clearer transaction-status information and create a knowledge-base article covering payment and top-up statuses.

## Expected Impact

- Fewer repetitive inquiries
- Faster customer support
- Better customer experience
- Easier issue tracking

> These are expected operational benefits, not measured business results.

---

# 04 — Improvement Loop

```text
Customer Complaint
        ↓
Observe Pattern
        ↓
Identify Root Cause
        ↓
Improve Information / Process
        ↓
Reduce Repetitive Issues
        ↓
Monitor Again
```

The objective is to treat Customer Service as a source of operational insight, not only as a channel for answering customer messages.
