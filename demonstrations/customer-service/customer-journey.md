# Customer Journey

## Overview

This document describes the customer journey used in the CRM and Customer Service demonstration for a gaming and digital top-up business.

The customer journey helps identify customer needs at each stage, from product discovery to repeat purchase. It also provides a structured approach to customer communication, transaction handling, issue resolution, and follow-up.

The objective is not only to complete transactions, but also to provide a clear, reliable, and positive customer experience before, during, and after a purchase.

## Customer Journey Map

```text
Customer Discovery
        ↓
Product / Price Inquiry
        ↓
Order & Payment
        ↓
Transaction Processing
        ↓
Issue Resolution
        ↓
Follow-up
        ↓
Repeat Purchase
```

## 01 — Customer Discovery

The customer first discovers the product or service through available sales and communication channels.

At this stage, the customer-service focus is to provide clear information and help the customer understand the available products.

Key activities may include:

- Providing product information.
- Explaining available services.
- Helping customers understand the purchase process.
- Answering initial questions.

The objective is to reduce uncertainty and help customers make informed purchase decisions.

## 02 — Product / Price Inquiry

Before placing an order, customers may ask about:

- Product availability.
- Product types and denominations.
- Pricing and payment options.
- Transaction procedures.
- Game account or player ID requirements.
- Other purchase-related information.

Customer service should provide accurate and easy-to-understand information so that customers know what to expect before proceeding with a transaction.

Clear communication at this stage can help prevent avoidable mistakes and reduce confusion during the ordering process.

## 03 — Order & Payment

The customer selects a product, submits an order, and completes the payment process.

Relevant transaction information may include:

- Transaction ID.
- Product purchased.
- Transaction amount.
- Payment method.
- Payment status.
- Customer identifier.
- Game account or player ID, where applicable.

The customer-service focus is to ensure that the order details are understood and that payment status can be checked when necessary.

A payment confirmation should not automatically be treated as proof that the digital product has been delivered successfully. Payment and product delivery may require separate verification.

## 04 — Transaction Processing

After payment, the transaction proceeds through order processing and digital product delivery.

Examples include:

- Mobile Legends diamond top-ups.
- Free Fire diamond top-ups.
- Electricity tokens and other digital products.

If a customer reports that a product has not been received, the investigation should establish the actual transaction state before deciding on the next action.

Relevant checks may include:

- Payment confirmation.
- Transaction status.
- Product and amount.
- Submitted game account or customer details.
- Order processing status.
- Product delivery result.

The objective is to identify whether the issue relates to payment, order processing, customer-provided information, or product delivery.

## 05 — Issue Resolution

Customer issues may arise at different stages of the transaction.

Common examples include:

- Payment pending.
- Top-up delayed.
- Incorrect player ID.
- Digital product not received.
- Transaction failed after successful payment.
- Product or payment inquiries.

The support process should begin by acknowledging the customer's concern and collecting the information needed for investigation.

### Investigation Approach

1. Acknowledge the customer's concern and show empathy.
2. Request the transaction details needed for verification.
3. Check the product, amount, payment status, and transaction status.
4. Verify the relevant customer or game account information.
5. Identify the likely source of the issue.
6. Explain the current status and available next steps.
7. Escalate the issue when further investigation is required.
8. Follow up until there is a clear outcome.

### Resolution Examples

**Payment successful, transaction pending**

Provide an update based on the available transaction status and follow the appropriate monitoring or escalation process.

**Payment successful, transaction failed**

Investigate the transaction and follow the applicable refund or reprocessing procedure.

**Incorrect player ID**

Verify the submitted information and determine whether the transaction can still be changed or cancelled. If the product has already been delivered, explain the available options based on the transaction status and service policy.

**Digital product not received**

Verify the payment and delivery status before deciding whether further processing, escalation, or another resolution is required.

A refund should be considered complete when the funds have been returned to the customer through the applicable process and the return has been confirmed. If a refund is still being processed, the case should remain open until there is a clear outcome.

## 06 — Follow-up

Follow-up is an important part of customer service, particularly when an issue cannot be resolved immediately.

The customer should receive appropriate updates without having to repeatedly ask for the status of their transaction.

Follow-up activities may include:

- Communicating investigation progress.
- Providing verified status updates.
- Explaining the next steps.
- Informing the customer when an issue has been resolved.
- Confirming the outcome when necessary.

The objective is to maintain transparency and customer confidence throughout the resolution process.

### Customer Service Principle

> Do not make the customer repeat the same information multiple times.

Information already provided by the customer should be used to make the investigation more efficient and reduce unnecessary communication.

## 07 — Repeat Purchase

A positive and reliable transaction experience can encourage customers to return for future purchases.

Customer retention is influenced by more than promotional activity. It also depends on:

- Transaction speed and reliability.
- Product availability.
- Competitive pricing.
- Ease of completing a purchase.
- Clear communication.
- Effective issue resolution.
- Consistent customer support.

Understanding these factors helps connect customer service activities with the broader customer lifecycle.

Customer interactions and transaction history can also provide useful information for identifying repeat customers, recognizing recurring issues, and improving the customer experience.

## Customer Experience Principles

The following principles guide the customer-service approach throughout the journey.

### Empathy

Acknowledge the customer's frustration and show understanding of their situation.

### Ownership

Take responsibility for following up on the issue until there is a clear outcome.

### Transparency

Provide honest, accurate, and understandable information about the transaction status.

### Proactive Communication

Keep the customer informed about meaningful progress instead of waiting for them to ask.

### Timely Resolution

Prioritize issues appropriately and take the necessary steps to resolve them as efficiently as possible.

### Trust and Reliability

Avoid making promises that cannot be guaranteed. Ensure that the customer understands the current situation and available options.

## Example Customer Interaction

**Customer:**

"Hi, I've made the payment, but my diamonds haven't been credited to my account yet."

**Customer Support:**

"Hi! I'm sorry for the inconvenience. I'd be happy to look into this for you. Could you please provide your Transaction ID or payment receipt and your player ID? I'll check the transaction status and keep you updated. Thank you for your patience!"

This response acknowledges the concern, requests relevant information, and establishes a clear next step without making assumptions about the cause of the issue.

## Summary

The customer journey provides a structured view of customer interactions from discovery to repeat purchase.

By combining accurate transaction verification, clear communication, appropriate issue handling, and consistent follow-up, customer service can support a more reliable customer experience.

This journey also provides a foundation for the CRM demonstration, where customer records, purchase history, case management, and customer lifecycle information can be connected to support more structured operations.
